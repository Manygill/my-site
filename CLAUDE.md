- Pricing: $10,000–$22,000/month

**Coaching** — 1:1 development for project managers and operations leaders sharpening strategic thinking, stakeholder management, and delivery discipline.
- Typical engagement: 3–6 months, bi-weekly sessions
- Pricing: $2,500–$4,000/month

**Workshops** — Structured, hands-on sessions equipping teams with frameworks to improve planning, prioritization, and execution. Built around your actual work, not generic theory.
- Typical engagement: Half-day to 2-day intensive
- Pricing: $3,500–$8,000 per workshop

## Lead Scoring Rules

Score every inbound lead as HIGH, MEDIUM, or LOW based on these criteria:

### HIGH Lead
All or most of these are true:
- Company is Series A or later, or 50+ employees, or an enterprise team
- Budget $10,000+/month or $20,000+ for a project
- Clear, specific pain point tied to program/project delivery, AI initiatives, or scaling
- Decision-maker or executive is in the conversation (not just an IC exploring)
- Urgency signal: launch deadline, exec pressure, failed previous attempt
- Strong fit for Fractional PM or Consulting engagement
- Industry: tech, SaaS, fintech, healthcare tech, enterprise software

### MEDIUM Lead
Some of these are true:
- Company is 10–50 people, or early-stage but funded
- Budget $3,000–$10,000/month or willing to discuss
- Pain point is present but somewhat vague
- Unclear if decision-maker, but engaged and specific
- Good fit for Coaching or Workshop
- Could grow into a larger engagement

### LOW Lead
One or more of these are true:
- Solo founder or pre-revenue startup
- Budget under $3,000/month or "no budget yet"
- Vague or exploratory — no specific pain point
- Just researching options, not ready to act
- Poor fit for any current service offering
- Requested something outside my scope (e.g., execution-only dev work)
